Which district to choose in Limoges?

Historic city centre, leafy Vanteaux, La Borie and its student campus, Isle and its health hub… Each district of Limoges has its own feel. This guide helps you choose to suit your situation.

The districts of Limoges

Choosing your district in Limoges

Begip manages properties in several areas of Limoges. Here's an overview of the most sought-after districts and what they offer.

01
Limoges city centre
Hyper-centre · Bénédictins · Les Halles · Cathédrale

The historic heart of Limoges brings together shops, restaurants, cinemas, the covered market (Les Halles) and Bénédictins station. Everything on foot. Ideal for people without a car or who want lively city life. Homes are often in older buildings, some renovated.

🚶 Everything on foot 🛒 Lots of shops 🚂 Bénédictins station T2 · T3
02
Vanteaux
South-west · Arts campus · Leafy residential

Vanteaux is one of the most pleasant districts of Limoges: green spaces, local shops, the Faculty of Arts and ENSAD at its heart. Popular with families, teachers and postgraduate students. A quiet, residential feel.

🌿 Green spaces 📚 Faculty of Arts 🏠 Quiet residential T2 · T3 · Houses

North-east · University campus · Student residences

Location

Limoges, an affordable city

Limoges is the capital of the Haute-Vienne, in the heart of the Massif Central. With around 130,000 inhabitants, it's a human-scale city where everything is quickly reachable — from the centre to the outskirts in under 20 minutes.

Its strengths: housing costs among the lowest of France's larger cities, a high quality of life (green spaces, rivers, low density), and a full range of universities and hospitals that make it a real city to build your life in.

The Begip network has been based in Limoges for over 30 years. Our on-the-ground knowledge of the buildings, districts and landlords lets us advise you effectively whatever area you're aiming for.

Choosing well

Three criteria to choose your district well

Before you decide, ask yourself these key questions.

Your workplace or place of study

Near the medical school? Choose Isle or the Marcland area. Faculty of Arts or ENSAD? Vanteaux is ideal. Hospital or IFSI? Isle again. University of Limoges main campus? City centre or La Borie.

Your lifestyle

Want everything on foot and a lively life → city centre. Looking for quiet and green spaces → Vanteaux or the northern areas. Have a car and want space → the outskirts or the Haute-Vienne.

Your budget

The city centre is slightly dearer than residential districts for the same size. Isle and the outlying towns offer the best value for money. Begip shows you properties across all price ranges.
