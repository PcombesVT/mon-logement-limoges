Frequently asked questions

The essentials to rent your flat in Limoges with confidence.

How do I rent a flat in Limoges with Begip?

▾
Contact Begip by phone on 05 55 33 97 50 or by email at agence@begip.fr, stating your search (type of flat, district, move-in date, budget). Our local team shows you the available homes, arranges viewings and supports you through the application, right up to the handover of keys.

What budget should I plan to rent a flat in Limoges?

▾
Limoges is one of the most affordable cities in France. Rent depends on size, district and fittings: from a studio at around €300/month including charges to a family-sized T4, via T2 and T3 flats. Homes are eligible for CAF housing benefit (APL or ALS), which reduces what you actually pay.

Are the flats let furnished or unfurnished?

▾
Most flats managed by Begip in Limoges are let unfurnished; some homes offer furniture provided, depending on the property. Tell us your preference when you enquire and we'll point you to what fits.

Do I need a guarantor to rent a flat in Limoges?

▾
A guarantor is generally required. Failing that, the Visale scheme (a free guarantee from Action Logement) is often accepted. Begip tells you the options available for your situation.

Are the flats eligible for housing benefit (APL / ALS)?

▾
Yes, subject to income conditions. For a private-sector flat, it is usually the ALS (social housing allowance) that applies, via the CAF. Begip guides you through the process when you sign the lease. See our guide to housing benefit.
