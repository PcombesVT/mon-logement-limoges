What is a T2?

Two rooms to live simply

A T2 flat (or F2) has a main living room — a living room or living/kitchen — and a separate bedroom, plus a bathroom and toilet. Typical size: 35 to 55 m² depending on the building and district.

It's the ideal format for a couple without children, a young professional wanting a separate sleeping area, or someone on a work relocation who wants to feel at home without managing a large space. In Limoges, T2 flats are found in every district, from the historic centre to the residential areas.

FAQ

T2 flats in Limoges — your questions

The most common questions from people looking to rent a T2 in Limoges.

What is the average rent for a T2 flat in Limoges?

▾
The rent for a T2 flat in Limoges is generally between €480 and €650/month including charges, depending on the district, the condition of the property and its fittings. Furnished T2 flats are slightly higher than unfurnished ones. Begip has a varied range across different areas of Limoges.

What's the difference between an unfurnished and furnished T2 in Limoges?

▾
An unfurnished T2 is let without furniture — minimum 3-year lease. A furnished T2 comes with bedding, a fitted kitchen and furniture — 1-year lease (9 months for students). Furnished rent is slightly higher but you can move straight in, with no furniture to buy.

Can you get ALS for a T2 in Limoges?

▾
Yes, subject to income conditions. For a T2 in the private market managed by Begip, it's usually the ALS (social housing allowance) that applies. The amount depends on your income, your household and the rent. Begip guides you through the CAF process at signing.

Which districts of Limoges for a T2?

▾
Begip has T2 flats in several districts, subject to availability: city centre (shops on foot, station), quiet residential districts, areas near the universities or the hospital. See our Limoges districts page for an overview.
