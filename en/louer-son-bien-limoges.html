Good to know

Frequently asked questions

The essentials to entrust the letting of your property in Limoges with confidence.

How do I entrust the letting of my home to Begip?

▾
Contact Begip by email at agence@begip.fr (or by phone), describing your property: type, size, district, condition, target rent. We provide a rent estimate, agree the option together (letting only or full management), then find and screen the tenant.

Letting only or full management: what's the difference?

▾
Letting means finding a reliable tenant and signing the lease (estimate, listing, viewings, application, check-in inventory). Full rental management adds ongoing follow-up: collecting rent, receipts, reviews, maintenance, check-out inventory. You choose the option that suits you.

How long does it take to let my property in Limoges?

▾
It depends on the property and the rent, but the Begip network of sites attracts tenants searching in Limoges every week. A correctly priced property usually lets quickly, which reduces your rental void.

How does Begip screen tenants?

▾
We check solvency, request supporting documents and accept suitable guarantees (Visale, guarantor). The aim: to present you reliable applications and secure your tenancy.

What are Begip's fees for a landlord?

▾
Begip charges clear fees, stated upfront, with no hidden costs. They depend on the option chosen (letting or management). See all management services on begip.fr.
