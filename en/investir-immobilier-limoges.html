Investing in rental property in Limoges

Affordable purchase prices, strong student and hospital rental demand, good yields: Limoges is one of the most coherent cities for a rental investment. And with Begip, you're supported from purchase all the way to management.

Why Limoges

A city built for rental investment

Limoges brings together the ingredients of a good investment: low entry prices, steady year-round housing demand, and a quality of life that keeps tenants in place.

💶
Some of the lowest prices

Limoges has some of the lowest purchase costs among France's larger cities. The low entry ticket lets you invest with a measured deposit and optimise the price-to-rent ratio — the basis of an attractive rental yield.

🎓
Steady rental demand

Around 20,000 students, a major teaching hospital and health sector, and a dynamic economy (nearly 17,000 companies, Ester Technopole): housing demand is strong and continuous, especially for studios and small units.

📈
An attractive yield

The "low prices + strong demand" pairing places Limoges among the cities with the best gross rental yields, particularly for studios and one-room flats near the campuses and centre — highly sought after every academic year.

🌿
A quality of life that retains tenants

Green spaces, a human-scale city, a moderate cost of living, a complete university and hospital offering: all of this keeps tenants and limits vacancy — a real plus for profitability over time.

Where & what to buy

Which property for which tenant?

The right investment depends on the tenant you're targeting — and each district of Limoges has its own demand:

Studios & one-room flats near the campuses (La Borie, Vanteaux, Isle/Marcland) and the centre: the student target, the largest in Limoges, with regular turnover and some of the highest rents per m². Two- & three-room flats in the centre or residential districts: young professionals, couples, hospital staff. Houses on the outskirts (Landouge, northern areas): families looking for space and stability.

To choose the right area, lean on our guide to the districts of Limoges: atmosphere, tenant profile and housing type for each.

Frequently asked questions

Investing in Limoges: your questions

Why invest in property in Limoges?
Limoges combines some of the lowest purchase prices among France's larger cities with steady rental demand: around 20,000 students, a major teaching hospital and health sector, and a dynamic economy. This price-to-demand ratio enables attractive rental yields, especially on smaller units near the campuses and the city centre.
What type of property should you buy to let in Limoges?
It depends on your target tenant. Studios and one-room flats near the campuses (La Borie, Vanteaux, Isle/Marcland) and the centre target students, who are very numerous in Limoges. Two- and three-room flats target young professionals and couples. Houses on the outskirts target families. Begip advises you based on the yield and demand of each area.
Can Begip manage my property after purchase?
Yes. That's precisely the benefit of a local agency: Begip supports you from finding the property to letting it and full rental management (finding a tenant, lease, inventory, rent collection, follow-up). You invest, we handle the rest — especially useful if you don't live in Limoges.
Do you need to live in Limoges to invest there?
No. Many investors buy in Limoges without living there, precisely because a local agency like Begip takes over on the ground: knowledge of the market and districts, property selection, letting and day-to-day management. You steer your investment remotely with peace of mind.
