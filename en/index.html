Renting in Limoges today

A snapshot of the Limoges rental market to frame your search — and why a local agency makes the difference.

🔑
Affordable rents

Limoges is among the cheapest French cities to rent in. A T2 flat rents well below big-city prices, for the same comfort.

📈
Demand that rises at the start of term

Students, hospital staff, young professionals and newcomers: rental demand climbs from June to September. Searching early means having the pick.

🗺️
From the city centre to the countryside

From a central T2 to a house with a garden in the Haute-Vienne, the offer is varied — provided you know the ground. That's been Begip's job for 30 years.
