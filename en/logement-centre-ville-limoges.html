Renting in the centre of Limoges

Living in the heart of Limoges means doing everything on foot: shops, the Halles covered market, the cathedral, the town hall, the Law Faculty and the station all minutes away. From the historic core to the town-hall area, Begip manages many flats in this sector where rental demand never lets up.

The city centre

The heart of the city, lived on foot

The centre of Limoges is the most convenient and liveliest part of the city: shopping streets, the covered Halles market, Saint-Étienne Cathedral, cinemas, restaurants and student life all come together here. You can easily do without a car — everything is a few minutes away, including the Bénédictins railway station.

It is also a historic centre, with characterful period buildings, many of them renovated. Begip manages a stock of well-located flats here, from the historic core to the town-hall area, sought after by couples, young professionals and small families who want to live in the heart of town.

The centre's sectors

The micro-districts of central Limoges

The centre of Limoges breaks down into several sectors, all a few minutes' walk from one another. Begip manages homes in each of them.

Renting a home at place des Carmes or in the medieval Boucherie quarter means choosing the most picturesque part of Limoges: old lanes, the Halles market and shops a stone's throw away, everything on foot. Begip manages homes here, from studios to two-bedroom flats.

Frequently asked questions

Renting in the centre — your questions

Where to rent in the centre of Limoges?

▾
The centre of Limoges is best lived on foot: the historic core (Boucherie quarter, place des Carmes, rue Haute-Vienne, rue de la Loi), the town-hall area (Hôtel de Ville, place Léon Betoulle), the law-courts district and the streets around the Law Faculty. Begip manages one- and two-bedroom flats here; contact the agency for availability to suit your budget.

Can you rent a home at place des Carmes in Limoges?

▾
Yes. Place des Carmes and its streets (rue de la Loi, rue Banc Léger, rue Haute-Vienne) are in the historic core of Limoges, in characterful period buildings. Begip regularly manages homes here, from studios to two-bedroom flats. See the available one-bedroom and two-bedroom flats, or contact the agency for a home at place des Carmes to suit your budget.

Can you rent near Limoges town hall?

▾
Yes, the town-hall area (Hôtel de Ville, place Léon Betoulle) is right in the heart of Limoges, steps from the shops and the Law Faculty. Begip manages flats in this central sector, ideal for car-free living.

Are there flats near the Limoges law courts?

▾
Yes, the law-courts district (towards rue de la Mauvendière and place d'Aine) is part of central Limoges. Begip manages flats there, valued for their central location and proximity to shops and services.
