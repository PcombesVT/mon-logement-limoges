The T3 in Limoges

Two bedrooms, a real home

A T3 flat (or F3) has two separate bedrooms, a living room — often open to the kitchen — a bathroom and a toilet. Typical size: 55 to 75 m². It's the go-to format for life as a couple with a child, a well-organised flatshare, or a household that needs a proper home office.

In Limoges, T3 flats are found in every district. Depending on the building, you'll find T3 flats with a balcony or terrace, with parking or a cellar included. Rent varies with location and level of finish.

Choosing between T2 and T3 in Limoges

Torn between a T2 and a T3? Here are the criteria that make the difference.

Choose a T2 if…

  • ·You're single or a couple without children
  • ·You want to keep your rent budget down
  • ·You don't need a separate office
  • ·You're here short-term (1–2 years)
  • ·You prefer a more affordable central district

FAQ

T3 flats in Limoges — your questions

The most common questions from people looking to rent a T3 in Limoges.

What is the average rent for a T3 flat in Limoges?

▾
The rent for a T3 flat in Limoges is generally between €600 and €800/month including charges, depending on the district, size and condition of the property. T3 flats with a balcony or parking are usually at the top of the range. Limoges remains a very affordable city compared with the big French cities.

Can you rent a T3 as a flatshare in Limoges?

▾
Yes, a T3 is well suited to a flatshare between two people. Begip can draw up a flatshare lease with or without a joint-liability clause. Each flatmate has their own bedroom, with a shared living room and common areas. Contact us to discuss the details.

Can you get housing benefit for a T3?

▾
Yes, subject to income conditions. ALS (social housing allowance) usually applies in the private market managed by Begip. For low-income households, the FSL (Housing Solidarity Fund) can also help with the security deposit. Begip guides you at signing.

Which districts of Limoges for a family T3?

▾
Families generally look for quiet districts with nearby schools, local shops and green spaces. The Vanteaux area, the wider city centre and some residential areas on the outskirts of Limoges are a good fit. See our guide to the districts of Limoges.
